How to Play

🎯

Select a player

Tap one of your team's players to select them. They'll glow yellow.

👉

Aim & shoot

Drag away from the player like a slingshot. A dotted line shows where they'll go. Release to launch!

⚽

Hit the ball

Your player slides across the pitch. If they hit the ball, it ricochets toward the goal. The ball bounces off walls and other players!

🏆

Score to win

Each team gets 5 shots. Knock the ball into the opponent's goal to score. Most goals wins!
